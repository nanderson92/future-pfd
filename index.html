Project thesis

The product is visible; the process system is hidden.

Most future technologies are discussed as products or vibes: clean cities, synthetic fuels, smart glass, autonomous factories, vertical farms, space habitats, flying cars, transparent displays, and fusion power.

Before any of them become normal, they need repeatable process windows, controlled materials, quality gates, reliable supply chains, safe deployment models, and economics that survive scale-up.

The bottleneck is not the concept. It is the system behind the concept.
